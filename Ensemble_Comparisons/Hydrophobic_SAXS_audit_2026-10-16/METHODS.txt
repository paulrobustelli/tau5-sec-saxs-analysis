Hydrophobic packing and SAXS hypothesis audit — 16 October 2026

SCOPE
9,866 original conformers: REST WT (1,866); IDPForge WT/AA (1,000 each);
bAIes Jeffreys 8A/seqsep2 WT/AA frozen prediction pools (1,000 each);
ASTEROIDS original WT/AA priors (2,000 each), with their historical 500-member
selected weights mapped by conformer ID. Newly running harmonic bAIes is NOT
included. All structures are common121 GP+AR330–448 including Cys; added Cys
for IDPForge/bAIes is a modeled terminal extension, not 121-residue dynamics.
Existing combined CS/SAXS weights are preserved unchanged.

DEFINITIONS
Heavy-atom coordinates, hydrogens removed consistently. Geometric C-alpha Rg
uses all121 CA atoms, including tags. Hydrophobic residues for this operational
analysis: ALA VAL ILE LEU MET PHE TRP TYR PRO CYS; GP tag residues excluded as
hydrophobic targets. Hydrophobic target atoms: sidechain C and S (excluding CA
and backbone C). Aromatic residues: PHE/TRP/TYR. Tyr OH and Trp N are NOT counted
as hydrophobic area targets. All heavy atoms, including polar atoms and tags,
can occlude target surface. These are geometric descriptors, not hydrophobic
energies or proof of thermodynamically stable cores.

SASA: MDTraj Shrake–Rupley, 1.4-A water probe, 240 sphere points per atom.
Nonlocal buried HSA for residue i = target-atom SASA in its unchanged +/-6
residue fragment MINUS target-atom SASA in the full structure. Thus surface
hidden only by residues more than6 positions away is counted; local helix
packing and adjacent chain occlusion remain present in BOTH calculations.
Sum over hydrophobic residues; units A^2. This is total lost accessible area,
not divided by2 as a two-partner interface area sometimes is. Positive burial
can result from polar as well as hydrophobic occluding atoms. No caps are added
or structures relaxed when making local fragments. Reference and full SASA use
identical atom radii and sphere directions. 12 evenly spaced conformers per
pool are independently checked with960 sphere points.

Hydrophobic contact: minimum sidechain C/S distance <5 A, |i-j|>6. Count unique
residue pairs. Coordination of a residue = number of distinct hydrophobic
partners, and mean coordination = twice pair count / number hydrophobic
residues. Aromatic-hydrophobic contacts are the subset with at least one
PHE/TRP/TYR; aromatic-aromatic pairs are also saved. These are sidechain contact
criteria, NOT ring-plane geometry or validated pi stacking. Sensitivities:
4.5/5/6 A cutoffs and sequence separations >3/>6/>10.
General tertiary contacts: minimum distance among ANY heavy atoms <5 A,
|i-j|>6, GP tags excluded as residues; 8/12 A counts also saved. No assumption
that all tertiary contacts are hydrophobic.

SAXS
Same canonical native Pepsi3.0 predictions as current common121 fits; dro5,
default Fraser radius, n80, noSmearing. Same experimental grid and sequence-
specific WT or AA curve. No new chemical shift or SAXS forward calculation.
Conditional plots show uniform group-mean raw curves, with ONE intensity scale
per group fit over the ENTIRE q range using experimental uncertainty; no offset
and no per-conformer normalization. Raw and scaled values are both saved.
Rg groups: <19,19–25,25–35,>=35 A. Other groups are within-pool tertiles; actual
cutpoints and populations are in CSV, and equal values can produce unequal
groups. Lower-ranked groups are not necessarily globally comparable cores.
Within-pool high/low HSA comparisons have equal counts in each2-A Rg bin.
Cross-method WT26–30 A comparison uses equal weight in each1-A Rg bin.
A diagnostic I(.15)/I(.03) ratio describes each calculated curve's shape;
correlations after regressing ranks on rank(Rg) and its square are descriptive,
not causal inference, formal independence tests, or individual-conformer fits.

NEW DIAGNOSTIC FITS (SEPARATE FROM EXISTING COMBINED FITS)
SAXS-only Borthakur dual/MaxEnt engine reused without modifying original code.
One global ensemble scale is optimized with the weights. All WT experiments
share the SAME frozen100-ns-block SAXS sampling SEM from the full REST prior,
and the same unit-per-point base regularization convention as the original
engine; regularization is calibrated separately for each experiment. This
standardized error model is a sensitivity experiment, not an assertion that
all pools have REST sampling errors. Experimental sigma is used for reported
chi-square, not substituted for the calibrated regularization SEM model.
Absolute Kish support stays >=0.1 times ORIGINAL pool size (REST186.6,
IDPForge/bAIes100, ASTEROIDS200), even after removing or adding structures.
Kish support is a weight-concentration measure, NOT an independent frame count.
There are no chemical shifts in these diagnostic fits. ASTEROIDS baselines
here are new exploratory SAXS-only Kish fits, not historical subset selection.
Inter-ensemble differences in published combined fits are not the baseline for
these comparisons. Reusing fitted SAXS is not independent validation.

Pool additions:160 unchanged REST WT donors, either Rg<19A (evenly spaced in
saved ordering) or Rg>=25A (all160 available). Compact donor choices also tested
with random seeds1 and2; numbers, targets, support constraints, and SEM stay
fixed. These are synthetic mixed-method pools, NOT new simulations or physical
population estimates. Only WT is mixed with WT. No WT-to-AA substitution.
Removal experiments retain original coordinates/predictions and require the
same absolute Kish support. Rg>=25A has only160 REST frames, fewer than the
required186.6, so that fit is infeasible by support alone and was not run.
Each completed fit passes finite-weight/support, gradient, scale stationarity,
and primal/dual tests. Independent einsum reconstruction verifies all saved
curves and effective support. Native BLAS emitted floating-point warnings;
all reported results are finite and independently reconstructed and checked.

LIMITATIONS
All findings are conditional on the supplied pools, forward model, chosen
error treatment, and contact/SASA definitions. Missing compact packing does
not prove that every compact REST structure is physically correct. REST is
enhanced sampling; saved-order changes are not physical kinetics. Known REST
cis-peptide populations and IDPForge peptide-geometry flags remain unresolved
physical-model limitations; no structures were silently discarded. Donor
replicates probe donor choice, not uncertainty in experiment or full molecular
sampling. Correlated frames are not treated as independent replicates for
p-values. A global SASA/coordination correlation does not uniquely identify a
particular native-like hydrophobic core or establish two discrete populations.
