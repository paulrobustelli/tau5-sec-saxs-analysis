Methods and interpretation¶

All new SAXS predictions use Pepsi3.0, dro5, default Fraser radius1.61 Å, q0–0.6 Å⁻¹, 601 samples, n80, no smearing, and one fitted ensemble intensity scale with no offset. No individual-conformer scales. Chemical shifts use SPARTA+; explicitly authorized reuse of original120-residue predictions for the terminally extended bAIes/IDPForge pools. Common chemical-shift observations cover AR330–446; missing observations are excluded and counts are reported.

Borthakur per-datatype calibration followed by global Kish regularization retains normalized Kish≥0.10. Sampling error uses fixed uniform-prior IID estimates for IDPForge and100ns independent blocks for bAIes/REST, with block-size scans. REST saved frame order is not physical kinetics. Numerical gradient/primal-dual checks validate the optimization; these fitted experimental data are not independent validation.

Construct and sampling provenance¶

IDPForge and bAIes dynamics originally contained120 residues. Cys448 was appended afterward with validated peptide geometry and clash checks; the terminal Pro carbonyl O was rotated when needed, and Pro OXT removed. This produces121-residue forward-model inputs, not newly equilibrated121-residue simulations. Original pools and results remain preserved. bAIes uses the exploratory8Å/seqsep2 selection sensitivity variant; it is not an exact paper reproduction or a validated upstream correction.

ASTEROIDS references preserve the existing500-member selected ensembles and2000-member priors. SAXS is reevaluated from archived canonicaldro5 intensities on the shared experimental grid; historical selection optimized hydration separately. These reference curves are not a new selection or Kish fit.

Geometry and support limitations¶

The supplied REST pool is compact; at normalized Kish≥0.10 its maximum possible mean Cα Rg is27.556 Å. This bound does not directly constrain a SAXS-derived Rg. REST reweighting can fit the supplied SAXS curve despite a lower Cα Rg; residual structure must still be inspected.

The polyproline audit found substantial incoming cis occupancy at REST Pro377 and a long trapped cis state at bAIes AA Pro372. IDPForge also contains peptide ω values outside cis/trans wells and positive Pro φ values. These are retained and reported, not silently removed. No cis/trans switching rates are inferred from REST. The student’s planned all-trans continuation is a separate future ensemble.

Preferred aggressive fits¶

These are the current preferred comparison results for the expanded WT and AA pools. Each is a single 500-member, equal-weight subset selection from the same expanded candidate pool. Carbon shift scales are 0.05 ppm, N is 0.25 ppm, and HN/Hα are 0.05 ppm. Summary tables¶

Rg and Sα are mean ± ensemble population SD (ddof=0), not standard errors. Rg is Cα geometric Rg, in nm. RMSDs are computed separately for each available nucleus (ppm); C means carbonyl C′, NH means HN. Missing nuclei are not imputed.

For sequence-separation threshold s, mean tertiary contact population is

P_s = [1/N_pairs(s)] Σ_(i<j, |i−j|>s) ⟨1[d_ij<1.2 nm]⟩.

The denominator includes all eligible unique residue pairs, including pairs that never contact. It is not the probability of having any contact. The numerical CSV also reports the expected number of contacts and pair count. Thresholds are strictly >5, >10 and >20.

The full-chain table contains whole-construct CS RMSDs and SAXS χ²/point. The R2–R3 table contains CS RMSDs restricted to AR391–446; SAXS metrics are deliberately omitted for this cropped segment. SAXS low-q weighting¶

Literature check: no explicit generic Guinier-upweighting rule was identified in the checked ASTEROIDS methods. Huang et al. (2014) use experimental SAXS errors and calculate 132 points through q≈0.2508 Å⁻¹ for their experimental range. Schwalbe et al. (2014) describe CRYSOL ensemble averages and genetic subset selection without specifying a special low-q multiplier in the main methods. This does not establish that every ASTEROIDS implementation treats q identically. In Naudi-Fabra et al. (2021), SAXS and extended-Guinier Rg were independent validation of an NMR/FRET-selected ensemble.

Our current objective sums [(a Icalc(q)−Iexp(q))/σ(q)]² over all fitted q points, without regional normalization or an extra Guinier weight. The number of points and their uncertainties therefore influence each region's contribution.

The following table partitions the existing objective into q<0.04, 0.04–0.10, 0.10–0.25 and q≥0.25 Å⁻¹. These are diagnostic bands, not assertions that q<0.04 is a validated Guinier window. Plots also show cumulative χ² and ln I versus q². The displayed low-q Rg is a weighted straight-line diagnostic over q≤0.03 Å⁻¹ (weights I/σ); inspect curvature and qmax·Rg before interpreting it. It is not the Cα Rg.

Low-q agreement is essential for overall size. Intermediate/high-q data still distinguish shapes and internal organization. A future controlled comparison could use J_SAXS = λ_low χ²_low/N_low + λ_rest χ²_rest/N_rest, with a separately documented total SAXS/CS balance, or restrict the fitting range and validate the withheld tail. Neither change has been applied here. Adding Rg as if independent of the same fitted SAXS curve would double-count information.

  • IDPConfGen, δ2D, SPARTA+, CRYSOL.

Sα: sum over seven-residue N/Cα/C/O windows of (1−(RMSD/0.10nm)^8)/(1−(RMSD/0.10nm)^12), with optimal rigid-body alignment; limit2/3 at RMSD0.10nm. The exact 50 original reference windows are used for AR391–446. Full-chain windows entirely within that segment use their matched references; outside it, the first seven-residue reference is the generic template. Contact means: generated conformers use nonperiodic distances; original MD uses the periodic treatment of the original analysis. Only measured restraints available for each sequence are used. Optimizer repeats and schedule variants are not independent candidate-pool replicates.
